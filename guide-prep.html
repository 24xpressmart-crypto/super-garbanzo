备餐计划告诉你今天或明天，每样熟食在每个时段要准备几份，不用再凭感觉。
The prep plan tells you how many of each cooked food to have ready in each time slot, today or tomorrow, so you no longer guess.

① 打开 · Open

首页 → 货品与库存 → 备餐计划Home → Goods & stock → Prep plan

  1. 在首页「功能」往下滑到 货品与库存，按 备餐计划。
    On Home, scroll the functions down to Goods & stock and tap Prep plan.
  2. 只有设定了备餐计划的店（现在是 BR26）才看得到这个按钮。
    Only stores set up for the prep plan (now BR26) see this button.
首页 · Home
② 看表 · Read the table

每一行一样食物，每一格一个时段One row per food, one column per time slot

  1. 上面选 今天 或 明天。开店前看今天，下班前看明天，方便先解冻、先备料。
    Pick Today or Tomorrow at the top. Check today before opening and tomorrow before you go home, so you can thaw and prepare ahead.
  2. 每一格是那个时段要准备好的份数。时段：开店到 10 点（≤10）、10–12、12–14、14–17、17–20、20 点后（20–24）。最右边 Day 是全天合计。「·」表示那个时段不用准备。
    Each cell is how many to have ready in that slot. Slots: opening to 10:00 (≤10), 10–12, 12–14, 14–17, 17–20, after 20:00 (20–24). Day on the right is the day total. "·" means nothing is needed in that slot.
  3. 卖得最多的排在最上面。
    The best sellers are at the top.
例子 · Example
ODEN STICK：≤10 要 4 支，10–12 要 10 支，12–14 要 5 支，14–17 要 13 支，17–20 要 2 支，全天 34 支。
ODEN STICK: 4 by 10:00, 10 for 10–12, 5 for 12–14, 13 for 14–17, 2 for 17–20; 34 for the day.

③ 包装货 · Packaged goods

只看要准备的食物，或显示全部Food to prepare only, or show everything

  1. 平时表上只有要动手准备的食物（熟食、面碗、套餐碗）。包装好的饮料、零食不用准备，所以不显示。
    Normally the table shows only food you have to prepare (cooked food, noodle bowls, combo bowls). Packaged drinks and snacks need no prep, so they are hidden.
  2. 想看全部，按最下面 显示全部商品（包括包装货）；再按 只显示要准备的食物 就回去。
    To see everything, tap Show all items (incl. packaged) at the bottom; tap Only food to prepare to go back.

④ 数字怎么来 · Where the numbers come from

按收银机过去 4 个星期的销售算Worked out from the last 4 weeks of till sales

  1. App 看过去 4 个同一个星期几（例如今天星期二，就看前 4 个星期二），每个钟头平均卖了几份，加起来再往上进位。
    The App looks at the last 4 same weekdays (on a Tuesday, the 4 previous Tuesdays), takes the average sold per hour, adds the hours up and rounds up.
  2. 卖完的时段数字会偏少：那天卖完了，收银机就没有记录。常常卖完的东西，请按经验多备一点。
    Slots where it sold out read low: once it sold out, the till recorded nothing more. For items that often sell out, prepare a little more from experience.
  3. 公共假期、活动、下雨天，客人会多或少，数字只是参考，店长可以调整。
    Public holidays, events and rain change the crowd; the numbers are a guide and the manager can adjust.
  4. 显示「这一天通常没有营业」：过去 4 个同一天都没有销售。显示「还没有这间店的每小时销售资料」：请告诉 HQ。
    "Usually closed on this day": no sales on the last 4 same weekdays. "No hourly sales for this store yet": tell HQ.
⑤ HQ · Settings

加门店、改要显示的类别Add stores, change the categories shown

  1. HQ 在「我的 → 设定」改 备餐计划的门店（例如 BR26,BR24），那间店的员工就会看到按钮。
    HQ changes Stores with a prep plan under Me → Settings (e.g. BR26,BR24); staff at those stores then see the button.
  2. 备餐计划要显示的类别：AutoCount 类别编号，默认 32 熟食、19 面碗、27 套餐碗。
    Categories shown in the prep plan: AutoCount category codes, default 32 prepared food, 19 noodle bowl, 27 combo bowl.
