先看这里 · Read first

为什么要确认Why we confirm

  1. 设备清单是从会计资料（AutoCount 资产记录）放进 App 的，按门店分好。请店长一样一样看：还在的确认，真的不在了才拿掉。
    The equipment list comes from the accounts (AutoCount asset records), split by store. The manager goes through it: confirm what is still here, and take off only what is really gone.
  2. 拿掉的设备不会删除，会计会看原因跟进（报废、搬店）。按错了 HQ 可以放回去。
    Nothing taken off is deleted; the accounts follow up the reason (written off, moved). HQ can put back anything tapped by mistake.
  3. 清单上没有、但店里有的设备，用 添加设备 自己加。
    Equipment in the store but not on the list: add it with Add equipment.
  4. 没有店长的门店由 HQ 确认。员工看得到清单，但由店长按。
    Stores without a manager are confirmed by HQ. Staff can see the list; the manager does the tapping.
① 设备 · Equipment

打开要确认的清单Open the list to confirm

按下面的 设备 → 门店设备。最上面黄边的卡说有几样要确认，按 开始确认。
Tap Equipment at the bottom → Store equipment. The yellow-edged card says how many items wait; tap Start confirming.

要确认 · To confirm
② 还在 · Still here

一样一样确认Confirm one by one

  1. 多件的（例 5 个 CCTV）先写还剩几件。
    For several (e.g. 5 CCTV), first enter how many are left.
  2. 按 还在。这一样就进入门店设备清单，冷柜冰柜会出现在温度记录里。
    Tap Still here. The item joins the store equipment list; chillers and freezers then appear in the temperature log.
  3. 只剩几件、少了的，HQ 会看到「只找到几件」并跟进。
    If fewer are left, HQ sees "only N found" and follows up.

③ 不在了 · Not here

真的不在了才拿掉Take off only what is really gone

按 不在了，选原因：坏了／已丢掉、搬去别的门店（选哪一间）、这间店从来没有、其他（要写明）。按 确定不在了。按 回到清单 就不改。
Tap Not here and pick why: broken / thrown away, moved to another store (pick which), never had it, other (write it). Tap Yes, not here. Back to the list changes nothing.

HQ 跟进拿掉的设备HQ follows up what was taken off

HQ 在 门店设备 下面看到 门店拿掉的设备：搬去别店的按 搬去，那间店就会收到要确认；按错的按 放回去。门店小标签上的 ⏳ 是还有几样没确认。
Under Store equipment HQ sees Equipment taken off by stores: for a moved item tap Move to and that store gets it to confirm; tap Put back for a mistake. The ⏳ on a store chip is how many still wait.
