排班的规矩Roster rules

  1. 每周开始前排好（最迟星期日晚上）。每天早上的考勤异常、迟到、缺勤都是照 App 里的排班算。
    Put the week in before it starts (Sunday night at the latest). Every morning's attendance check (late, absent, missed punch) uses the roster in the App.
  2. 一个人一班一格。分两段的（例 8AM-1PM 在 BR09、晚上在别店）就排两个班，各选对的门市。
    One shift per box. A split day (e.g. 8AM-1PM at BR09, evening at another store) is two shifts, each at the right store.
  3. 过夜班：结束时间比开始早就是到第二天，例 17:00-02:00、18:00-09:00。半夜 12AM 开始的班（例 12AM-10AM）排在第二天的日期，开始 00:00。
    Overnight: an end earlier than the start runs into the next day, e.g. 17:00-02:00, 18:00-09:00. A shift starting at midnight (12AM-10AM) goes on the next day's date, starting 00:00.
  4. 休息、请假不用排。请假请员工在 App 申请（年假要提前 7 天），批准后考勤不会算缺勤。
    Days off and leave are not shifts. Staff apply for leave in the App (annual leave 7 days ahead); once approved it is never counted as absent.
  5. 支援别店：排在去支援的那间店。员工要先设好「支援门市」才排得进（HQ 在人员里设）。
    Helping another store: put the shift at the store they go to. The person needs that store in their cover stores first (HQ sets it in Staff).
  6. 整周一次上传最快（下面 ④）。传错了改好文件再传，同一天会被新文件换掉。
    Uploading the whole week is fastest (④ below). If something is wrong, fix the file and upload again; each day is replaced by the new file.
店长 ③ 排班 · Roster

加一个班、改一个班Add or change one shift

按首页的 排班，或在 我的 往下到 排班管理。蓝色格子是已排的班，+ 是空的。
Tap Schedule on Home, or scroll to Schedule Management in Me. Blue cells are shifts; + is empty.

  1. 用 ‹ 本周 › 翻到要排的那一周。表格可以左右滑，看到星期五到星期日。
    Use ‹ This week › to show the week. Swipe the grid sideways to see Friday to Sunday.
  2. 点那个人那一天的格子。
    Tap that person's cell for the day.
排班管理 · Week grid
  1. 点一个班次模板（早班 09:00-18:00、中班 14:00-23:00、夜班 23:00-07:00），马上存好。
    Tap a shift template (Morning, Afternoon, Night); it is saved at once.
  2. 时间不一样，就改开始时间 / 结束时间，再按 保存排班。
    For other times, set Start / End and tap Save Shift.
  • 格子里已有班：打开后按 删除 就删掉。A cell with a shift: open it and tap Delete.
  • 今天以前的班店长不能加也不能删；要改找 HQ。Managers cannot add or delete shifts before today; ask HQ.
  • 复制上周 把上一周的班整周抄过来（已有的不会重复）。Copy last week copies the previous week's shifts (no duplicates).
  • 名字旁边有「支援」的，是别家店来帮忙的人。A Cover tag marks someone from another store helping here.

店长 ④ 上传排班 · Upload a week

整周排班一次上传Put the whole week in at once

先翻到要排的那一周，在排班管理按 上传排班。HQ 先点店号。
Show the week first, then tap Upload schedule in Schedule Management. HQ picks the store first.

  1. 按 下载本周模板，得到 schedule-BR19-2026-09-28.csv 这样的文件，用 Excel 或 Google Sheets 打开。A 列名字、B 列工号，C 到 I 列是星期一到星期日，已经有 App 里现在的班。
    Tap Download this week's template; open the file (e.g. schedule-BR19-2026-09-28.csv) in Excel or Google Sheets. Column A is the name, B the staff code, C to I Monday to Sunday, already holding the shifts in the App.
  2. 只改星期的格子，存成 .xlsx 或 .csv。回到这里按 选择文件 选它。（电脑上也可以把整个表格连第一行复制，贴在下面的框里。）
    Change only the day cells and save as .xlsx or .csv. Back here, tap Choose file and pick it. (On a computer you can also copy the whole table, header row included, and paste it into the box.)
  3. 按 检查。这一步还没有保存，只是先给你看会改什么。
    Tap Check. Nothing is saved yet; it only shows what would change.
格子怎么写 · What to put in a cell
09:00-18:00（也可写 9am-6pm、0900-1800）· 过夜 23:00-07:00 · 两段 09:00-13:00 / 17:00-22:00 · 班次名 早班 中班 夜班 · 不上班 OFF 或 休（当天原有的班会删掉；请假仍要在 App 申请）· 空白 = 不改。
09:00-18:00 (or 9am-6pm, 0900-1800) · overnight 23:00-07:00 · two shifts 09:00-13:00 / 17:00-22:00 · a shift name (Morning / Afternoon / Night) · day off OFF (removes that day's shift; leave is still applied for in the App) · empty = no change.
上传排班 · Upload
  1. 看预览：最上面是「新增 · 删除 · 不变」几格；每一行是「那天原本 → 保存后」，— 表示原本没有班。
    Read the preview: the top line counts Add · Remove · Unchanged; each line is "before → after"; — means no shift before.
  2. 没问题就按 保存排班。App 保存后会再读一次、逐格核对：看到「✅ 已保存并逐项核对」就完成，员工会看到新班。看到「⚠️ 以下没有生效」就截图给 HQ。
    If it is right, tap Save schedule. The App reads the week back and checks each day: ✅ Saved and checked means done and staff see the new shifts. ⚠️ Not applied: send a screenshot to HQ.
本店名单里找不到 · Not in this store
名字或工号对不上，那一行没导入。用模板里的写法再传一次。
The name or code doesn't match; that row is skipped. Use the spelling from the template and upload again.
看不懂的格子 · Cells not understood
例如 9-9。这些格子不导入，其他照常；改好再传。
E.g. 9-9. Those cells are skipped, the rest go ahead; fix and upload again.
已过去的日子不会改 · Past days are not changed
今天以前的班已经用来算出勤和工资，店里不能改；需要改找 HQ。
Days before today were already used for attendance and pay; ask HQ if one is wrong.

传错了就改好文件再传一次，同一天会按新文件换掉。每天早上的考勤异常是按 App 里的排班判断的，请在那一周开始前上传好。
Uploaded something wrong? Fix the file and upload again; each day is replaced by the new file. Morning attendance exceptions are judged against the App's roster, so upload before the week starts.
