打卡手册Clock-in guide for store staff

上班、下班都要在 App 打卡。每次打卡要拍一张照片，App 会记下时间、门店和位置。
Clock in and out in the App at the start and end of every shift. Each clock needs a photo; the App records the time, store and location.

从 10 月 1 日起：没有照片就不能打卡。照片只能用相机当场拍（自拍或拍店面），不能从相册选。
From 1 October: no photo, no clock. The photo must be taken live with the camera (a selfie or the shop). Photos from the gallery are not accepted.

① 登录 · Log in

第一次在这部手机登录First time on this phone

  1. 打开 App，输入工号（例如 M00076）或邀请纸条上的邀请码，按 下一步。
    Open the App, type your staff code (e.g. M00076) or the invite code from your slip, and tap Next.
  2. 输入你的 PIN（密码），按 登录。第一次会请你设一个 PIN。
    Enter your PIN and tap Log in. The first time, you are asked to set one.
  3. 之后这部手机会记住你，下次只要输入 PIN。别人的手机不要用你的名字登录。
    The phone then remembers you; next time only the PIN. Never log in on someone else's phone with your name.
输入工号 · Staff code
看到「这个账号已停用」？表示手机记住的是你的旧账号。App 会自动回到输入工号的画面，用你现在的工号重新登录就好。
See "This account is no longer active"? The phone remembered your old account. The App goes back to the staff-code screen; sign in again with your current staff code.
忘了 PIN？请店长或 HQ 帮你重设。连续输错 5 次会锁 15 分钟。
Forgot your PIN? Ask your manager or HQ to reset it. Five wrong tries lock the account for 15 minutes.

② 上班打卡 · Clock in

上班打卡：先拍照，再按大圆按钮Clock in: photo first, then the big round button

  1. 按下面的 打卡 分页。看一下打卡门店是不是你今天上班的店（支援别间店就选那间）。
    Tap the Clock tab. Check Store shows where you work today (pick the other store if you are covering).
  2. 按 📷 自拍 拍自己，或按 📷 拍店面 拍店里（收银台、门口都可以）。相机会打开，当场拍一张。
    Tap 📷 Selfie for yourself, or 📷 Photo of the shop for the store (counter or entrance). The camera opens; take the photo now.
上班打卡 · Clock in
  1. 照片会显示在下面。拍得不好就按 重新拍照，不要就按 移除。
    The photo shows below. Tap Retake photo if it is poor, or Remove.
  2. 按大圆按钮 上班打卡。手机问是否允许位置，请按「允许」。
    Tap the big Clock In button. When the phone asks for location, tap Allow.
  3. 看到「已打卡」就完成了。记录会出现在下面的「打卡记录」。
    You are done when it confirms. The punch appears under Records.

✓ 可以 OK

  • 自拍，脸看得到
    A selfie with your face visible
  • 店里的照片（收银台、门口、货架）
    The shop: counter, entrance, shelves

✗ 不行 Not OK

  • 从相册选旧照片（App 不给选）
    An old photo from the gallery (the App won't allow it)
  • 拍天花板、口袋、全黑
    Ceiling, pocket, or a black picture

The pictures in this guide show the English screens; the Chinese and Malay screens have the same buttons in the same places. The EN / BM / 中 button at the top right switches language.

③ 下班打卡 · Clock out

下班打卡：一样先拍照Clock out: the same, photo first

  1. 上班打卡后，大圆按钮会变成黑色的 下班打卡。
    After clocking in, the big button turns black: Clock Out.
  2. 下班时一样先按 📷 自拍 或 📷 拍店面 拍一张，再按 下班打卡。
    At the end of the shift take a photo the same way, then tap Clock Out.
  3. App 会问「确定现在下班打卡？」，按确定。这是防止不小心按到。
    The App asks Clock out now?; confirm. This stops accidental taps.
  4. 夜班跨日：隔天早上下班照样按下班打卡（上班后 20 小时内都算同一班）。
    Overnight shifts: clock out the next morning as usual (up to 20 hours after clocking in counts as the same shift).

打卡记录里的 📷 表示那次有照片；店长和 HQ 看得到。
The 📷 on a record means it has a photo; managers and HQ can see it.

④ 补卡 · Missed punch

忘了打卡，或手机拍不了照Forgot to clock, or the phone can't take the photo

  1. 按下面的 我的 分页，按 补卡申请（首页也有「补卡」按钮）。
    Tap the Me tab, then Missed punch (the home screen has a shortcut too).
  2. 选上班或下班，填日期、时间、门店，在「事由」写清楚原因（例如：手机相机打不开）。
    Choose In or Out, fill in the date, time and store, and write the reason (e.g. phone camera would not open).
  3. 按 提交申请。店长或 HQ 批准后才算数。
    Tap Submit. It counts once the manager or HQ approves it.
补卡是例外，不是天天用。常常补卡，店长和 HQ 会看到。
Missed punches are the exception, not a habit; managers and HQ see how often they are used.

⑤ 常见问题 · FAQ

遇到这些情况When this happens

按了拍照，相机打开后 App 就关掉重开 · The App restarts when the camera opens
手机内存不够。先关掉其他 App 再试；也可以改按「拍店面」（用后镜头）。还是不行，就用补卡申请，并告诉店长换一部手机打卡。
The phone is low on memory. Close other apps and try again, or use Photo of the shop (rear camera). If it still fails, send a missed punch and tell your manager so you can clock on another phone.
按「上班打卡」出现「请先拍照再打卡」 · "Take a photo first, then clock"
还没拍照，或照片被移除了。先按「自拍」或「拍店面」拍一张。
No photo yet, or it was removed. Take one with Selfie or Photo of the shop first.
手机问要不要给位置，我按了「不允许」 · I tapped "Don't allow" for location
到手机的「设置 → 浏览器 / App → 位置」打开。没有位置的打卡会显示「无定位」，不在店附近会显示「超出范围」，店长和 HQ 都看得到。
Turn it on in the phone's Settings → browser / App → Location. A punch without location shows No location, and one away from the store shows Out of range; managers and HQ see both.
为什么不能从相册选照片？ · Why can't I pick from the gallery?
公司规定打卡照片要当场拍，防止用旧照片代打卡。
Company rule: the clock photo must be taken on the spot, so nobody can clock for someone else with an old photo.
密码一直错 · My PIN keeps failing
确认登录的是你自己的名字（按 ✕ 或「不是我」可以换人）。忘了 PIN 请店长或 HQ 重设。
Check it is your own name (tap ✕ or "Not me" to change). If you forgot the PIN, ask your manager or HQ to reset it.
看到蓝色的「有新版本」 · A blue "new version" bar appears
按它更新。旧版本可能没有最新的打卡按钮。
Tap it to update. An old version may not have the latest clock buttons.
