App 新功能What's new in the 24 Xpress App · 20 Oct 2026

这几天 App 多了这些功能。更新后（重新打开 App 或刷新网页）就能用。
New this week. Reopen the App (or refresh the page) to get them.

所有人 · Everyone

📷 点照片放大Tap a photo to zoom

点任何一张单据照片，会全屏显示。按 ＋ / − 放大（最大 6 倍），或点两下，或双指放大。按「关闭」或手机返回键回来。
Tap any document photo: it opens full screen. Use ＋ / − (up to 6×), double-tap or pinch. Press Close or the Back button to return.
收银员 · Cashiers

🧮 数钱计算器Cash counter

写「交接班」时，点「🧮 数钱计算器」，每种纸币和硬币填几张，总数自动算好。它只是计算器，不会保存；数好后把同一个总数输入 POS 关班。
When you write a shift handover, open "🧮 Cash counter", enter how many of each note and coin, and the total is added for you. It saves nothing; type the same total into the POS close-shift.
所有人 · Everyone

↩ 撤回我送错的单据Withdraw a Snap you sent by mistake

在「拍单据」里，你自己送出、还没人处理的单据（例如供应商对账单），可以按「↩ 撤回这张」。记录会保留，不会被删除。
现金单、刷卡单、ePay 报表、法律/政府信件、报价单，不能自己撤回，请告诉 HQ。
In Snap documents, a paper you sent that nobody has handled yet (e.g. a supplier statement) has "↩ Withdraw this". The record is kept, nothing is deleted. Cash slips, card slips, ePay reports, legal/government letters and quotations cannot be withdrawn by you: tell HQ.
收货 · Goods receive

🛠 收货还没进 AutoCount 时，可以直接改货号／数量Fix item / quantity before AutoCount

打开那张收货 → 按「🛠 改货品／数量」→ 选正确的货品、写原因 → 保存，系统会自己送去 AutoCount。已经进了 AutoCount 的，要在 AutoCount 里改。
Open the delivery → "🛠 Fix items / qty" → choose the right item, give a reason → Save; it then goes to AutoCount by itself. Once it is already in AutoCount, change it in AutoCount.
会计检查员 · Accounts checker

❓ Query 要选问题类型A Query now has a problem type

按「❓ Query」时选：货号 / 日期 / 数量 / 价钱 / 其他。货号和日期要由会计在 AutoCount 更正，门店看到「会计会更正，你不用做什么」，也不会收到提醒。更正后按「🛠 我已在 AutoCount 改好」。Home 的「🛠 N 张要在 AutoCount 更正」会列出还没改的。
When you press "❓ Query" choose Item code / Date / Quantity / Price / Other. Item code and date are corrected by accounts in AutoCount: the store sees "accounts will correct this" and gets no push. After correcting press "🛠 Amended in AutoCount". Home shows "🛠 N to amend in AutoCount".
Runner

⚠ 重复记录提醒Double-entry warning

一小时内，同一间店、同样的现金金额又要记一次时，App 会先问「是不是同一次？」。按「取消」就不会重复记。
If you try to log the same store with the same cash within an hour, the App asks "is this the same visit?". Press Cancel to avoid a double entry.
HQ

首页新卡片New Home cards

⛔ 被卡住的收货（有一行没有货号，点进去直接改）· 💬 员工问题在等回复 · 拍单据列表显示等了几天，红色＝7 天以上，并写明为什么还没结案。
⛔ Held receives (a line has no item code; tap to fix) · 💬 staff questions waiting for a reply · Snap list shows how many days each paper has waited (red from 7 days) and why it is still open.
