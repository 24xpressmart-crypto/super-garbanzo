About this build. Every zone name and description below is placeholder copy, and the sphere you are looking at is a generated calibration grid — bearing marks every 30°, a horizon line, and zenith/nadir labels — so the controls can be checked before the real imagery lands. Send the panorama and the zone details, and both get swapped in. 关于此版本：下方所有区域名称与说明均为占位内容，当前球面是自动生成的校准网格（每 30° 一个方位刻度、水平线、天顶与天底标记），便于在真实图像到位前检查操作。提供全景图与区域资料后即可替换。
