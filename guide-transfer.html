门店之间调货 · Transfer stock between stores

24 Xpress App · 全体门店员工 · All store staff · App 2026-10-04.143

先看这里 · Read first

规矩Rules

  1. 送货的店在 App 开调货单，收货的店在 App 点收。点收后 App 自动在 AutoCount 开调货单，数量按收货店点到的。不用再在 AutoCount 手动打调货单。
    The sending store makes the transfer in the App; the receiving store counts it in the App. After the count, the App creates the AutoCount transfer for the quantities the receiving store counted. No more keying transfers in AutoCount by hand.
  2. 送货的人不能自己点收，要由收货店的同事点。收到的不能比送出的多。
    The sender cannot confirm their own transfer; a colleague at the receiving store counts it. You cannot receive more than was sent.
  3. 少了就写实际收到的数量，没到写 0。少的部分 HQ 会自动收到通知跟进。
    If short, enter what actually arrived (0 if none). HQ is told about any shortage automatically.
  4. 咖啡馆（BR24、BR26）不是调货，请继续用「记到咖啡馆」。
    The cafés (BR24, BR26) are not transfers; keep using "Book to café".
① 打开 · Open

收货页 → 门店调货Goods receive → Store transfers

在 收货 页最上面按 门店调货（送出／收货），或按首页的「调货单等你确认」卡。
On the Goods receive page tap Store transfers (send / receive) at the top, or tap the "transfers waiting" card on Home.

调货 · Transfers
② 送出 · Send

开调货单Make a transfer

  1. 选送去哪间店。
    Pick the store.
  2. 打货名、货号或扫条码，点一下加入，写数量。
    Type the name or code, or scan; tap to add; enter the quantity.
  3. 按 送出，把货和调货单号（XF…）一起交给对方。
    Tap Send and hand over the goods with the transfer number (XF…).
送出 · Send
③ 点收 · Receive

货到了：点数确认Goods arrived: count and confirm

一样一样点，写实际收到的数量，按 确认收到。列表会显示「AutoCount 已过账」。如果显示「还没进 AutoCount」，按 再送一次。
Count each item, enter what arrived and tap Confirm received. The list then shows "Posted in AutoCount". If it says "not in AutoCount yet", tap Send again.
